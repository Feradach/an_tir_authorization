Two-person authorization requirement: effective November 1, 2026

Effective November 1, 2026, An Tir requires two people to sign off on a fighter's first authorization in a discipline, or when the fighter has had no valid authorizations in that discipline for more than one year. This is required by the Society's two person integrity rule.

  • An authorizing marshal who is qualified to conduct authorizations in that discipline.
  • A second person who holds any current, valid authorization in the same discipline. This person may be another marshal or an authorized participant.

These must be two different people, neither of whom is the person being authorized. Both take part in the authorization process, with the second person serving as a witness and providing concurrence.

This requirement does not apply to Siege or Youth Combat. Existing approval requirements for marshal authorizations remain unchanged.

Creating a new account

Creating your own account is the preferred way to get started. Go to the login page, select Create an Account, and fill in your details. You will receive an email with your username and a link to set your password.

Marshals with the required authority are also welcome to create accounts for new people using the add-fighter action on their home page. See the FAQ account-creation instructions for more details.
